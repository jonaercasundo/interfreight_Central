# Phase 5 Department Workflows

## Commercial handoff

Sales may register a shipment only from an accepted quotation with an active commercial baseline and only for a quotation owned by that Sales user. Operations, Transport and Forwarding users may register direct-origin work within their authorized department. A direct job is explicitly marked as lacking a commercial baseline.

## Operations

Operations coordinates master job execution, customs entry and permit records, document verification, operational milestones and exceptions. Customs release does not automatically close the master shipment.

## Transport

Transport records multiple legs, dispatch and pickup/in-transit/delivery states, delivery details and private POD uploads. POD review is separately authorized. Completing a trip does not change the master job status.

## Forwarding

Forwarding records bookings and carrier schedules, container/seal data where applicable, and forwarding milestones. Air records use flight information; sea records use vessel/voyage information.

Departments share the master register and timeline while retaining separate assignments and status records. Accounting and Finance have read-only access to shipment data and the Phase 4 funding readiness summary.
