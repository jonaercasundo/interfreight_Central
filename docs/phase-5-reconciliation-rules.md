# Phase 5 Reconciliation Rules

Dashboard counts use distinct master shipment records. Transport trips, forwarding bookings, containers and deliveries are separate operational records and must not be summed as shipments. Department reconciliation compares the master register with distinct jobs assigned to each department.

The Cost & Funding Status view links approved quotation estimates and Phase 4 approved allocations by matching requirement source and currency. It reports estimated requirement, allocated amount and remaining estimate by currency. No currency conversion is performed, and estimates are not spend commitments, actual expenses, invoices or payments.

Direct-origin jobs have no accepted commercial baseline and remain pending commercial/funding review. No budget allocation is inferred. Phase 5 does not create invoice, payment, actual expense, profitability, or financial close records.
