# Budget utilization policy

By approved allocation and currency, consumption is verified actual expense (including verified adjustments, less verified reversals) plus open approved advance reservations plus remaining verified disbursed advance balance. A liquidated expense is included as actual while the same amount reduces advance outstanding, so total consumption remains stable. Disbursement is not an expense.

Approved budget is the approved Phase 4 budget line; allocated budget is the allocation amount. Remaining allocation is floored at zero and any negative pre-floor difference is shown as over-budget exposure. Open commitments are unavailable in Phase 1–5 and are explicitly excluded. Available-to-allocate additionally reserves approved pending and outstanding advances. Currency totals are never combined.
